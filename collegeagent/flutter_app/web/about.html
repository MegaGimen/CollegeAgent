CollegeAgent

CollegeAgent is an intelligent AI-powered academic assistant designed specifically for college students. Our platform seamlessly connects with your university's Canvas Learning Management System (LMS) to extract your courses, modules, and assignments, turning them into a personalized knowledge graph.

Core Features & Functionality

  • Canvas Integration: Automatically syncs your courses, assignments, and reading materials from Canvas.
  • AI Conversational Assistant: Chat with an AI that understands your syllabus, due dates, and university policies.
  • Automated Scheduling: Exports deadlines and classes directly to your personal calendar.

Why We Request Google Data

To provide you with the best time-management experience, CollegeAgent requests access to your Google Calendar. We use the calendar.events permission to:

  • Automatically create calendar events for your upcoming homework deadlines and exams.
  • Sync your Canvas course schedule into your daily agenda.

Transparency note: We only write events to your calendar based on your explicit commands. We do not sell or share your calendar data with third parties.
